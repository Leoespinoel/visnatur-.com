Made to order

A Signature piece, made to fit.

Any piece from the Signature line can be made to order: the sleeves, the length or the fit adjusted to you, then cut and sewn by hand in Mauritius. €25 per piece, ready 3–4 weeks after the order.

  1. 01

    Choose your adjustments

    On any Signature piece, choose Made to order and set the sleeve, the length or the fit. €25 per piece.

  2. 02

    It is made for you

    The measurements are confirmed by email, then the piece is cut and sewn by hand in Mauritius. Allow 3–4 weeks from the order.

  3. 03

    It arrives

    Pressed, folded into tissue and sent express from the island. Repaired for free, for life.

What can be adjusted

Cut to you.

Shirts, knits and tees
Sleeve length and body length, up to 4 cm shorter or longer.
Trousers and shorts
Cut to your inseam, and the waist taken in or let out up to 2 cm.
The fit
As cut, a little slimmer, or a little more relaxed.
Anything else
A note with the order, answered before anything is cut.

Made to order is for the Signature line. Limited edition pieces come in small numbers and sell as they are. €25 per piece, ready 3–4 weeks after the order.

Shipping & delivery

In days, or weeks when it is made for you.

Signature & collections
In stock in Mauritius and dispatched within 1–2 working days.
Made to order
3–4 weeks from the order, with an email when the piece is cut and when it ships.
Delivery
Shipped express from Mauritius, tracked, 4–7 working days to Europe. Free over €150, otherwise €15.
Where we ship
The EU, Norway, Iceland, Switzerland and the United Kingdom. Pieces are made outside the EU, so import duties and VAT may be collected on delivery; we show an estimate at checkout.
Packaging
Recycled card and paper. No plastic, no polybag.

Trousers and shorts are sized by waist in inches (28–38); made to order, they are cut to your inseam. Socks come in EU 39–42 and 43–46.

Our fits are relaxed but not oversized. If you are between sizes, go down for polos and knitwear and up for shirts and outerwear. Still unsure? Write in with the measurements of something that fits well, and the right size comes back by email. Or have the piece made to order.

Every style has one measurement sheet for all its colourways, so every piece comes out the same. Garments come in M, L and XL, sampled in L. The caps come in S, M and L, sampled in M; the socks in EU 39–42 and 43–46; the sunglasses in one size.

Finished measurements in centimetres, sunglasses in millimetres. A ½ measurement is taken flat across one side; HPS is the high point of the shoulder; ± is the tolerance. These are working specs, to be confirmed against the first samples.

Questions

Asked often.

What is made to order?+

A Signature piece adjusted to you: the sleeves, the length, the inseam, the waist or the fit. Choose Made to order on the piece, set the adjustments, and it is cut and sewn for you by hand in Mauritius.

How long does it take?+

Made to order takes 3–4 weeks from the order, then 4–7 working days express to Europe. Signature and Limited edition pieces as they are leave Mauritius within 1–2 working days.

What does it cost?+

€25 per piece on top of its price, whatever is adjusted.

Can a Limited edition piece be made to order?+

No. Limited edition pieces come in small numbers and sell as they are. Once they are gone, they are gone.

Can I cancel or return?+

Signature and Limited edition pieces can be returned within 14 days of delivery, unworn and unwashed. A made to order piece can be cancelled within 48 hours of the order for a full refund; after that its fabric has been cut.

What if it doesn't fit?+

Signature and Limited edition pieces can be exchanged for another size within 14 days of delivery. If a made to order piece doesn't match the measurements agreed, it is remade at no cost.

Where are the pieces made?+

In Mauritius, in small workshops visited in person. The island has made knitwear and tailoring for the world's best-known labels for fifty years, and the Indian Ocean around it is part of why the fifth exists. The workshop list is published as the partnerships are confirmed.

Repairs?+

Free, for life. Send it back, it is mended and sent home. Postage to the workshop is yours; the return is ours.
