Privacy.

What we collect

When you order, we collect your name, email, shipping address and the details of what you bought. Payment details are collected and stored by Shopify, never by us.

What we use it for

To make and ship your order, to contact you about it, and, if you subscribe, to send occasional letters. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Cookies

This site uses no tracking cookies. Your bag is stored in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us until you check out.

Processors

Shopify (our shop and checkout, which also processes payments), Hostinger (hosting), and our shipping carriers receive only what they need to do their part.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may access, correct or delete your data at any time. Write to hello@visnaturae.com.
