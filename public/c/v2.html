1/5 of profits to the wild

1/5 of each year's profit goes to conservation around Mauritius: reefs, native forest and mangrove coast. Small places where a little money goes a long way.

  • <1%

    of the ocean floor is coral reef, yet reefs support an estimated quarter of all known marine species. Protect a little reef and you protect a lot of sea.

    Source: NOAA

  • 2%

    of Mauritius is still native forest. Small sums here have already pulled the kestrel, the pink pigeon and the echo parakeet back from extinction.

    Source: Convention on Biological Diversity

  • Up to 4×

    more carbon per hectare is held in mangrove forests than in most other tropical forests. Replant a little shoreline and you lock away a lot of carbon.

    Source: USDA Forest Service
