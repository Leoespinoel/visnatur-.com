FAQ

Asked often.

The lineup

What is the club?+

The membership. It is free: points on every order and on a few other things, three spots to move up through, and rewards to spend the points on.

How do I join?+

Make an account with your email under Become a member. No password: a code comes by email, and the account is your membership.

Can I leave?+

Any time. Write and the membership is closed. The points go with it.

Points

How are points earned?+

On every order, and for everything under Level up: joining, the letters, Instagram, bringing a friend, your birthday and Members Only.

When do points count?+

From the day they show in your account. Join before then and the welcome points are waiting.

Do points expire?+

After twelve months without an order or a reward.

What about returns?+

Points from a returned order come off again. A remake in another size keeps them.

What is the leaderboard?+

Three tables: the current drop, this month and all time, each in order of points earned. First names only, and only members see it.

Spots

How many spots are there?+

Three: Voyager, Explorer and Trailblazer. Your spot follows the points earned in the last twelve months.

Can I lose my spot?+

No. Once it's yours, it stays yours.

Members Only

What is Members Only?+

A paid subscription on top of any spot. All of it goes to the wild; none of it stays with the brand. Members get discounts at the clubs and events Vis Naturæ partners with.

What does it cost?+

The price is announced with the first partner clubs. Write in to hear it first.

Stores

Does the club work in a store?+

Not yet: Vis Naturæ is online only for now. Pop-ups and partner clubs are listed under Stores the day they are confirmed.
