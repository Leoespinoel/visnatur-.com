The lineup

For the people who come back.

Free to join. Points on every order, three spots to move up through, and rewards worth spending them on. Your spot follows the points earned in the last twelve months, and nobody moves down. Members Only goes further: a subscription that goes entirely to the wild.

  1. 0–999 points

    Voyager

    1 point for every €1

    • A first look at new pieces before they go on the site
    • Points on your birthday
    • Invitations to the community runs and open events

  2. 1,000–2,999 points

    Explorer

    1.25 points for every €1

    • Everything a Voyager gets
    • Invitations to VN nights
    • Repairs to the front of the queue

  3. 3,000+ points

    Trailblazer

    1.5 points for every €1

    • Everything an Explorer gets
    • A welcome gift, made only for Trailblazers
    • A say in what is made next
    • A seat at the members' dinner

Sponsorship

We back people who go all in.

Vis Naturæ sponsors athletes and ambassadors, artists, and clubs, teams and events. Not the biggest names: the ones who go all in, then rest like they mean it. We read every application and answer within two working days.

Athletes & ambassadors

Surfers, kiters, sailors, golfers, climbers, runners: people who compete hard, or simply go all in at something, and care about the places they do it in.

Apply as an athlete
What we give & what we look for+

What we give

  • Pieces made for you, in your size, through the season
  • Your story in our films and on these pages, in your own words
  • A say in the next pieces we make, tested where you train

What we look for

  • Commitment you can see, at any level
  • Care for the coast, the mountains and the water you use
  • Honesty: wear what you like, and say what you don't

Artists

Painters, photographers, illustrators, filmmakers, musicians and makers: people whose work starts in the sea, the forest or the island, and who keep at it.

Apply as an artist
What we give & what we look for+

What we give

  • Pieces made for you, in your size, to work and live in
  • Your work in our films and on these pages, credited by name
  • A chance to make something with us: a print, a lining or a label

What we look for

  • Work that comes from the places we build
  • A practice you keep at, in any medium, at any stage
  • Honesty: make what you mean, and tell us what you think

Clubs, teams & events

Golf and sailing clubs, rugby and surf clubs, regattas, tournaments and teams of every kind, in Mauritius and across Europe.
